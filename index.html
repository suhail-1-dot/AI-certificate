Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming industries by enabling machines to learn, reason, and make decisions. This workshop gives you a solid foundation in modern AI concepts, from classical machine learning to large language models.

You will explore how AI systems are built, trained, and deployed, and understand the ethical considerations behind them.

What Will You Learn?

  • Core principles of machine learning and deep learning.
  • How neural networks and transformers work.
  • Natural Language Processing and large language models (LLMs).
  • Computer vision and pattern recognition.
  • AI ethics, bias, and responsible deployment.
  • Real-world AI applications and case studies.
  • Hands-on tools: Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch basics.

Machine Learning Foundations

Machine learning is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data. You'll learn about supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning, and how to evaluate model performance.

Neural Networks & Deep Learning

Deep learning uses multi-layered neural networks to solve complex problems. We'll cover perceptrons, backpropagation, CNNs, RNNs, and the transformer architecture that powers modern AI.

Natural Language Processing (NLP)

NLP allows machines to understand and generate human language. Topics include tokenization, embeddings, attention mechanisms, and fine-tuning large language models.

Computer Vision

Computer vision enables machines to interpret images and video. Learn about convolutional networks, object detection, and image generation.

AI Ethics & Responsible AI

As AI becomes more powerful, ethical considerations are critical. We discuss bias, fairness, transparency, privacy, and the societal impact of AI systems.

Real-World AI Applications

AI is used in healthcare, finance, autonomous vehicles, recommendation systems, and creative tools. You'll analyze case studies and understand how AI projects are structured.
